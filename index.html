10 FAQ

Questions worth asking

What does Tarkbyte do?

Tarkbyte is a software consultancy. We build custom software, websites and mobile apps, and AI solutions such as RAG systems and agentic workflows. We also offer design and SEO, tech advisory, developer resources and career services.

Who do you work with?

Startups, established businesses and individuals with an idea. We also offer deploy ready software for schools, shops and hospitals.

Do you offer career services?

Yes. Resume building, mock interviews, interview preparation and job consultancy for tech and management professionals.

Which technologies do you use?

Python, Django and FastAPI on the backend, modern web frontends, and Android and iOS apps. For AI work we build RAG based systems, agentic AI and automation with LangChain and LangGraph.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. After the discovery call you receive a proposal with a clear timeline and milestones, and we share progress after every sprint.

Can your industry software be customised?

Yes. The school, retail and hospital systems run from day one and can be customised to your workflows.

Do you support the software after launch?

Yes. We stay with you after launch with maintenance, updates and improvements.
